# Design System: BLIU Atelier Storefront

## 1. Visual theme and atmosphere

Follow the client-approved Google Stitch project **BLiU Luxe Storefront Redesign**. The site is an Indian handloom and couture storefront with a quiet editorial mood: generous negative space, large garment photography, restrained gold labels, and serif display typography. The gateway introduces the two collection realms; each realm then has its own landing and shopping flow.

## 2. Color palette and roles

### Shared / BLIU Essentials
- **Ivory Canvas** (`#FAF9F5`) — primary background and open editorial space.
- **White Surface** (`#FFFFFF`) — product and content surfaces.
- **Atelier Gold** (`#D4AF37`) — restrained highlights, small labels and selected states.
- **Graphite Ink** (`#111111`) — headlines and primary text.
- **Stone Rule** (`#E5E0D8`) — quiet dividers and borders.
- **Muted Stone** (`#797676`) — secondary copy and metadata.

### BLIU Luxe
- **Obsidian Canvas** (`#0A0A0A`) — persistent Luxe background.
- **Nocturne Surface** (`#111111`) — secondary dark sections.
- **Antique Gold** (`#D4AF37`) — active navigation, key labels and price accents.
- **Soft Ivory** (`#EAE6DF`) — Luxe body text and inverted surfaces.

Keep BLIU Luxe dark with gold accents across its landing, archive, product, account and support screens. Essentials uses the light ivory palette. Do not introduce extra accent colors.

## 3. Typography

- **Display / editorial:** Playfair Display, with restrained tracking and italic emphasis where appropriate.
- **Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans, readable line-height and comfortable measure.
- **Labels / navigation:** DM Mono or a comparable compact mono face, uppercase with generous tracking.
- Keep collection names, product names and section headings prominent. Do not reduce important copy to tiny metadata.

## 4. Components

- **Gateway:** BLIU wordmark, short brand statement, then two large image-led entry panels for BLIU Essentials and BLIU Luxe.
- **Collection landing:** editorial collection heading and description, the collection-only scope line, four image-led categories, then a curated garment grid.
- **Product cards:** portrait garment photography, compact craft label, clear garment name and INR price. Recommendations stay within the active realm and show clothing only.
- **Product detail:** dominant, well-framed garment image with alternate views, product information, Indian size selection, size-guide link, purchase actions and separate craft details.
- **Controls:** restrained borders and gold/green active states by realm; visible keyboard focus and tactile active state.
- **Footer:** editorial BLIU signature, discovery links, client care and studio links.

## 5. Layout and responsive behavior

- Preserve the Stitch page order and image-to-copy proportions; contain desktop layouts to a wide centered canvas.
- Use spacious two-column compositions where shown, and let garment imagery carry the visual weight.
- At mobile widths, stack gateway panels, category tiles and product detail columns without horizontal overflow.
- Keep collection context visible on Shop All and filter only products in that collection.
- Preserve image aspect ratios and use deliberate focal positioning; never stretch a garment image.

## 6. Motion and interaction

Keep motion quiet and short. Use opacity and transform for gentle image and control feedback. Never let hover effects shift text or alter a garment crop. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## 7. Never do

- Never rename BLIU or merge the Essentials and Luxe realms.
- Never show a light theme on BLIU Luxe.
- Never show shoes, bags, jewelry or other accessories in product recommendations.
- Never replace individual garment photography with a collage or unrelated model image.
- Never fabricate product certifications, payment/security guarantees, delivery promises, or customer contact details.
- Never copy personal customer, address, payment or account data from prototype screens into the storefront.
- Never crowd product pages, shrink major headings or distort garment photos.

## Source asset note

The local project currently contains a four-look composite image rather than the individual product and embroidery images shown in Stitch. Treat it as a temporary source; replace it with the corresponding approved garment and craft photographs before launch to reproduce the Stitch image placement faithfully.
